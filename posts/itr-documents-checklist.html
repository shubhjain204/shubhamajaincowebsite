Documents required for ITR filing in India

Use this as a preparation checklist before sharing documents with your CA.

A complete document set helps your return get filed accurately and reduces follow-up. The exact list depends on your income sources, investments, loans and transactions during the year.

Basic documents

  • PAN, Aadhaar and bank account details.
  • Form 16, salary slips and details of previous employment, if any.
  • Form 26AS, Annual Information Statement and Taxpayer Information Summary.
  • Interest certificates from banks and loan statements.
  • Investment proofs for deductions and exemptions.

Extra documents for business or professional income

  • Sales, expenses, bank statements and cash records.
  • GST returns and books of accounts, where applicable.
  • TDS details and professional fee receipts.
  • Details of fixed assets, loans and outstanding receivables.
